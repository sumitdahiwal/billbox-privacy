BillBox Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

BillBox ("the App") is a bill and expense management application designed to help users organize bills, track payments, manage recurring expenses, view expense information, and export bill records.

1. Information We Collect

BillBox is designed to store bill information locally on the user's device.

Information entered into the App may include:

  • Bill name
  • Bill amount
  • Bill category
  • Due date
  • Payment status
  • Recurrence information
  • Notes entered by the user
  • App preferences such as dark mode

BillBox does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, phone number, or email address to use the core features of the App.

2. How Information Is Stored

Bill information and application preferences are stored locally on the user's device using local storage.

BillBox does not operate a user account system or require users to upload their bill records to a BillBox server.

3. CSV Export and Sharing

BillBox provides an option to export bill information as a CSV file.

When the export feature is used, the generated file is passed to the device's available sharing or download mechanism. The user chooses where or with which application the exported file is shared.

Once the file is shared with another application or service, that application's own privacy policy and data practices apply.

4. Information Sharing

BillBox does not sell, rent, or intentionally provide users' locally stored bill information to BillBox for advertising or marketing purposes.

Bill information may leave the device when the user intentionally uses the CSV export and sharing feature.

5. Third-Party Services and Libraries

BillBox uses third-party software libraries to provide application functionality.

These libraries may process technical information according to their own policies and the behavior of the operating system or platform on which BillBox is used.

BillBox does not intentionally use third-party services to collect users' bill records for advertising or marketing.

6. Data Security

BillBox stores bill information locally on the user's device. Users should protect access to their device and operating system because locally stored information may be accessible to someone who has access to the device.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because BillBox stores bill information locally, the user controls the data stored by the App on their device.

Users may delete individual bills using the deletion functionality provided by the App.

Users may also remove locally stored App data through the operating system's application settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to the behavior of the device and operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

BillBox is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core bill-management functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be made available through the privacy policy URL used for the Google Play listing.
